🌅 Lição 7 · Daily Routine — O Tema que Você Mais Vai Falar

Narrar sua rotina completa com naturalidade — juntando Present Simple, horas e as preposições de tempo in/on/at.

1. As Horas

  • What time is it? — It's seven o'clock (7:00)
  • seven fifteen (7:15) · seven thirty (7:30) · a quarter to eight (7:45)
  • a.m. (manhã) / p.m. (tarde/noite)
  • at 7 a.m. = às 7 da manhã

2. in / on / at — A Regra dos Três Tamanhos

PreposiçãoPara...Exemplos
athoras e pontosat 7:00, at night, at lunch, at the weekend (UK)
ondias e datason Monday, on Fridays, on May 5th
inperíodos maioresin the morning, in July, in 2026, in the summer

💡 Decore os quebra-regras: at night, mas in the morning/afternoon/evening.

3. Verbos e Frases da Rotina

wake up (acordar) · get up (levantar) · take a shower (tomar banho) · get dressed (vestir-se) · have breakfast/lunch/dinner (tomar café/almoçar/jantar) · brush my teeth (escovar os dentes) · go to work (ir ao trabalho) · start/finish work (começar/terminar) · take a break (fazer pausa) · get home (chegar em casa) · go to bed (ir dormir)

4. A Rotina-Modelo (Leia em Voz Alta 3x)

I wake up at 6:30 in the morning. I take a shower, get dressed and have breakfast. I start work at 9 and take a break at noon. In the evening, I study English for one hour. On Saturdays, I don't work — I usually see my friends. I go to bed at 11 p.m.

5. Erros de Brasileiro (Não Caia)

  • "in Monday" → on Monday ✓
  • "at the morning" → in the morning ✓
  • "in 7 o'clock" → at 7 ✓
  • Refeições usam have: have breakfast (não "take")
