📅 Lição 10 · Past Simple — Destravando o Passado

Contar o que aconteceu: verbos regulares (-ed), os irregulares mais frequentes, was/were e o auxiliar did.

1. was / were (Passado do To Be)

  • I/he/she/it was · you/we/they were
  • Negativa: wasn't/weren't
  • Pergunta: Was it good? Were you there?

2. Verbos Regulares: +ed

  • work → worked · play → played · study → studied (consoante+y) · stop → stopped (dobra)
  • Pronúncia do -ed:
    • /t/ depois de som surdo (worked)
    • /d/ depois de sonoro (played)
    • /id/ depois de t/d (wanted, needed)

4. Negativa e Pergunta: did + verbo no normal

  • I didn't go to the party. (não "didn't went")!
  • Did you see the game? — Yes, I did.

💡 O did absorve o passado; o verbo volta à base — mesma lógica do does na Lição 3.
