📖 Lição 14 · Storytelling — Contando Histórias de Verdade

Juntar TUDO (passado + conectores + tempo) para contar uma história com começo, meio e fim — e conhecer o Past Continuous para dar cenário.

1. Past Continuous — A Ação em Andamento

was/were + -ing — a ação que estava em andamento quando outra aconteceu:

  • I was working when you called. — Eu estava trabalhando quando você ligou.
  • It was raining, so we stayed home.
  • While she was cooking, I was setting the table.

O par perfeito: Past Continuous (cenário) + Past Simple (evento)

  • when costuma vir com o evento
  • while com a ação longa

2. Os Sequenciadores

  • One day / Last year... (abertura)
  • First..., Then..., After that..., Later... (sequência)
  • Suddenly... (virada)
  • Finally / In the end... (desfecho)
  • Luckily / Unfortunately... (avaliação)

3. História-Modelo (Leia em Voz Alta, Depois Releia sem Ler)

Last Friday, I was driving home when my car stopped in the middle of the street. First, I tried to start it again, but nothing happened. Then I called a mechanic. While I was waiting, it started to rain! Luckily, a friend saw me and helped me push the car. In the end, the problem was small — and cheap. We laughed a lot.
