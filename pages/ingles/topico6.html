🔗 Lição 6 · Connectors — Costurando Ideias

Sair da frase solta: ligar ideias com and, but, or, because, so, when — o salto de "tarzan" para "falante".

1. Os Seis Essenciais

ConectorUsoExemplo
andadiçãoI like coffee and tea.
butcontrasteIt's small but comfortable.
oralternativaTea or coffee?
becausecausaI'm tired because I work a lot.
soconsequênciaI'm tired, so I'm going to bed.
whentempoCall me when you arrive.

💡 because x so — o par que confunde: because introduz a causa; so introduz o resultado.

I stayed home because it was raining. = It was raining, so I stayed home.

2. Bônus Úteis

  • also (também) — I also like pizza.
  • too (também, no fim) — I like pizza too.
  • then (então/depois) — I ate, then I slept.
  • before/after (antes/depois) — I eat before I sleep.
  • first (primeiro) — First, I wake up.
  • finally (finalmente) — Finally, I go to bed.

3. Exemplos

  • I work from home, so I save time. — Trabalho de casa, então economizo tempo.
  • She speaks English and Spanish, but she doesn't speak French.
  • We can walk or take a bus.
  • When I wake up, I drink coffee first. Then I check my email.
  • He is happy because today is Friday.
